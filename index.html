Install SplitGram on iPhone

Use SplitGram full-screen offline like a native iOS app

  1. 1
    Open this page in Safari on your iPhone and tap the Share button in the bottom bar.
  2. 2
    Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen (Agregar a pantalla de inicio).
  3. 3
    Tap Add in the top-right corner. SplitGram will launch full-screen and work offline!
✓ Done!
